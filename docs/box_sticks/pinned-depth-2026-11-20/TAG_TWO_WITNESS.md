2026-11-20, read-only (live wells after the v4 cutover; the geologist's grid files). Follows `TAG_SIZING.md`. Nothing

What changed against the height-only count:

## Limits

- A grid is a witness, not a verdict; where both witnesses fail a well they can still share a cause (the height bands and
  the grids both lean on Wolfcamp A structure).
- Midpoint sampling, not station by station; wells within 25 ft of a surface are never called off by the grid.
- 212 wells have a grid answer and no WCA_1 control; 2 % of wells are outside the grids.
- Wells with no depth evidence are not judged.
