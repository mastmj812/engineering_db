For cutoffs T = 2021-01-01 and 2023-01-01 the step-2 metric (r = ½ mi, c = ¾ mi) is rebuilt from the pool laterals online before T, with 12-mo oil known only when 12 months had elapsed by T. Every config is scored on the wells online at/after T whose lateral sits ≥ 50 % outside the T core and within 3 mi of it, excluding infill of holes that were open at T. A well is performing at ≥ 0.70 × the T interior median 12-mo oil/ft (the 2×2 rolled threshold), rolled below it; wells without a cohort 12-mo are not scored. Captured = ≥ 50 % of the lateral inside the would-be extent. J = share of performing wells captured − share of rolled wells captured. Selection rule (fixed before the run): best pooled J among configs with floor ≥ 880 ft (next-row rule), §6 pool-wide reference unless local wins by > 0.02; ties within 0.02 go to the smaller added area.
