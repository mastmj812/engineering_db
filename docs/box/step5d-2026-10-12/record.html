Gas has no ref_mult: its level is set per location (D30 local GOR + D46). Di is nominal per year, 1-yr effective alongside. EUR = raw 50-yr integral.

5. Flags

  • Rung 3 is terminal whatever its n (D45): the across-benches sandwich factor rests on 10 wells (oil 0.786).
  • D42 on sandwiches: 7 WCA sandwich wells have a same-pool side. That side became infill and the other side's one-sided relation (topfill / underfill) was kept. D42's text names topfill / underfill only; this is my reading for sandwiches.
  • The D42 relabel runs on the RAW (class, parent bench) before rare pairs pool to '<class>|other'. 5c's pooled view relabelled after pooling, so a rare same-pool pair could hide inside '|other'. That ordering is one source of the 5c-vs-5d pooled-view differences in §2.
  • ref_mult (D43) uses the factors of record with ACTUAL cum-12 weights (5c's mult_cum12 convention). The stored params stay the reviewed as-built curve, and the reference curve is derived from them (qo, qi × ref_mult). The alternative is to store the reference curve itself. Your call.
  • The gas GOR factors are relative to the reference class, but the D30 local level is the median of mixed-scenario neighbours. In WCA, reference-class wells run 1.041× (mean) / 1.000× (median) their local level (n 4,232). α_PUD = α_local + ln f_GOR (D46 as written) leaves that offset in. Reported, not changed.
  • BS2_S has factors but no TC areas and no curves (the D4 pilot is WCA), so box.type_curve holds WCA only.
