5. Gate asks

  1. Confirm the QC'd well sets: BS3_S 3,041 producing horizontals (2,365 first-producing ≥ 2016; 1,658 in the D9 cohort) and BS3_C 1,818 (1,651; 1,003), built with the class rule of record + your gate-1 card verdicts, no new cards. Recommend: confirm.
  2. Uncalibrated swap classes (17 flags in 9 small classes, e.g. BS1_S↔BS3, WCB_1/WCC/OTHER→BS3_S): the rule keeps the tag. Applying every consensus flag touching the family moves the cohort median 12-mo oil/kft +0.0 % (BS3_S) / −1.0 % (BS3_C). Recommend: no calibration cards; tags kept.
  3. Pooling (D19 / D33 pattern): BS3_S and BS3_C are depth-separable where they co-occur (BS3_C sits a median 496 ft shallower; bands merge in 3 % of shared neighbourhoods vs 36 % for WCA_1/WCA_2; order inverted in 1 %), only 55 sand↔carbonate ambiguous wells, and within the same 5-mi block BS3_C makes 0.90× BS3_S 12-mo oil/kft [90 % CI 0.83–0.95] with the same GOR (1.03×) and shape (cum-24/cum-12 0.99×). Only ~half of each bench's cohort has the other within 1.5 mi. Recommend: do NOT pool. Two benches through extents (two D27 envelopes), analogs (deal-intake same-bench rule, as WCA vs BS2_S) and acceptance. Pooling would let one bench's development stand in for the other's in an extent, and would mix a ~10 % level difference into analog cohorts.
  4. Re-tag drift since gate 1 (flag, not resolved): 91 wells tagged BS3 in the live warehouse are held by a frozen Phase 1 pool under their gate-1 tag (43 in WCA: 22 WCA_1 / 10 WCA_2 / 11 WCXY → now BS3_S or BS3_C, 27 in the cohort; 48 in BS2_S), mostly Novi re-tags. Under D84 a frozen well is never re-decided, so they stay out of the BS3 sets (no well counts twice). It also means ~43 now-BS3 wells sit in the run-7 WCA analog pool. Recommend: keep D84 here; take the re-tag question (should a warehouse re-tag move a frozen well?) to the next WCA / BS2_S pool refresh or step 13, not this step.
