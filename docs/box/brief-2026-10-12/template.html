WCA type-curve areas

The 35 areas of record (box.tc_area v1). Area values are cohort statistics: D9 filter, first prod ≥ 2016, lateral 6,000–13,000 ft. Hover or tap an area to see its card.

Novi bo/ft = Novi's 30-yr oil EUR ÷ lateral, a vendor forecast (step-4 gut-check map). BOX = the stored record oil curve's raw 50-yr integral per 1,000 ft ÷ 1,000 (cohort mean, not P50, before risking and F(k)). The horizons differ, so compare the patterns, not the levels. GOR = the area's actual cohort 12-mo GOR. Areas with one operator ≥ 70 % of wells are pad-scale (D9 confounding accepted).

How GOR is handled

GOR in the WCA runs as an east-to-west maturity trend. It cuts across the oil-performance areas. Switch the map above to GOR to see it. We use one rule set for it:

1 · Areas are oil-builtThe TC areas cluster on 12-mo oil/ft. GOR does not shape them. 16 of 35 areas mix GOR regimes.
2 · Gas = ratio mode (D30)ln GOR = α + β·Np, fitted against cumulative oil on the area's mean series. Gas = that ratio × the oil curve. There is no separate gas Arps.
3 · Level is localEach location shifts α to its own neighborhood's GOR: the median 12-mo GOR of the 15 nearest cohort wells. The area supplies only the shape, β.
4 · Risked gas (D46)Child wells run gassier than their neighborhood, so gas gets a GOR factor on α. Infill: 1.024 WCA / 1.063 BS2_S.

Step 6: the hindcast (acceptance test)

  • Rebuild steps 4–5 using only wells online before the 2025Q3 report cutoff, inside the same extents. That means areas, curves, factors, F(k) and local GOR levels all come from pre-cutoff wells.
  • Holdout: the ~1,742 wells kept in the trimmed 2025Q3 slices (sql/42 / sql/43), with actuals through 2026-08.
  • Prediction per well: area curve × lateral × factor (dev scenario computed by us, sql/50) × F(k). Gas = ratio at the well's pre-cutoff local GOR. Novi = its own 2025Q3 forecast.
  • Pass criteria (§8): per bench-area with ≥ 10 holdout wells, for cum-6 and cum-12, oil and gas. BOX mean error must be within ±10 % and |BOX bias| < |Novi bias|. BOX's P10–P90 error band must be no wider than Novi's. Both must hold on a majority of qualifying areas. Reference: Novi cum-6 oil bias was −24 % in the Delaware.
  • Deliverable: an acceptance page with bias and P10–P90 band per bench-area, BOX vs Novi, and a pass/fail verdict. A fail means diagnose (areas, factors, or ladder) and re-run. No app work starts until it passes.
Two decisions needed before scoring (§9):
  • Does "majority" count bench-areas, or weight them by holdout wells?
  • Gas acceptance: same ±10 % / no-wider-band / majority rule as oil, with pass = oil AND gas? Or does gas get its own thresholds?
