Pecos has zero picks on every reference surface: it is kept out of the basin panel and shown last, labelled NO PICKS; its lines are modelled, so read it as where wells land, a first hint for picking. Nothing here re-tags a well: the split depths are candidate naming rules for Michael and the geologist to decide.
