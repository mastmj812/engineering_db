Each Delaware producing horizontal's formation_blueox tag is scored against the grid interval it should sit in (tag top → next surface down). Wells with a Novi survey are scored at every lateral station (sql/64), the rest at the midpoint tvd_ft. agree = majority of the lateral inside the tag interval. A disagreement means the tag OR the grid is wrong; step 2 attributes which. Evidence wells only: not a planned survey, ≥ 6 mo online, (survey or non-round tvd_ft), and landing depth ≥ 4,000 ft (shallower = a data error on a scored bench).
