Each Delaware producing horizontal's formation_blueox tag is scored against the grid interval it should sit in (tag top → next surface down). Wells with a Novi survey are scored at every lateral station (sql/64), the rest at the midpoint tvd_ft. agree = majority of the lateral inside the tag interval. A disagreement means the tag OR the grid is wrong; step 2 attributes which. Evidence wells only: not a planned survey, ≥ 6 mo online, (survey or non-round tvd_ft), and landing depth ≥ 4,000 ft (shallower = a data error on a scored bench).

Geology decisions applied (2026-10-20): WOLFCAMP-C and WOLFCAMP_D are suspect (to be relabelled / re-picked) — WCC and WCD are surface_suspect, and a WCB_2 'deep' call (base = C top) is too. AVA_1 = Middle Avalon (operator tops ~110 ft above AVALON2) has no surface — no_surface until one is built; AVALON1 = top Upper Avalon, AVALON2 = top Lower Avalon. Pecos County: Wolfcamp A–D and AVALON2 have zero picks — masked; XY, Bone Springs and Bell/Cherry/Brushy stay scored there with caution. Agreement shares are over agree / shallow / deep only; the raw geometric verdict is kept as verdict_raw in the CSV.
