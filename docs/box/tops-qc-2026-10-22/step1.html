Tops vs tags — step 1: sampling, datum, agreement

Each Delaware producing horizontal's formation_blueox tag is scored against the grid interval it should sit in (tag top → next surface down). Wells with a Novi survey are scored at every lateral station (sql/64), the rest at the midpoint tvd_ft. agree = majority of the lateral inside the tag interval. A disagreement means the tag OR the grid is wrong; step 2 attributes which. Evidence wells only: not a planned survey, ≥ 6 mo online, (survey or non-round tvd_ft), and landing depth ≥ 4,000 ft (shallower = a data error on a scored bench).

Geology decisions applied (2026-10-20, revised 2026-10-22; grids = the geologist's V5): one WCA bench = WCXY + WCA_1 + WCA_2, XY top → B1 top (rule A; no WCA_1/WCA_2 sub-split, rule C). WCC = his B2 → C interval (relabel) — that interval also holds most WCB_2 landings, see step 3. WCD = his C → D, held (unscored) for log review; WOLFCAMP_D stays suspect. Rule B: the XY top is the BS3_S / Wolfcamp boundary, Lea and Ward pending his review (flagged, still scored). AVA_1 = Middle Avalon (operator tops ~110 ft above AVALON2) has no surface — no_surface until one is built; AVALON1 = top Upper Avalon, AVALON2 = top Lower Avalon. Pecos County: Wolfcamp A–D and AVALON2 have zero picks — no_control (kept out of agreement, shown on the step-2 maps and counted in its picks-needed table: revised 2026-10-21); XY, Bone Springs and Bell/Cherry/Brushy stay scored there with caution. Agreement shares are over agree / shallow / deep only; the raw geometric verdict is kept as verdict_raw in the CSV.
