The 275 PUDs within 500 ft of a "producer": why sql/21 did not realize them

2026-10-15 · read-only investigation (no DDL, no writes) · Novi 2026Q3 · Delaware WCA_1 / WCA_2 · source list: BOX 7c runs/box7c/run.pkl (near_pdp_ft < 500) · per-pair table: pud_near_producer_audit_2026-10-15.csv

The match logic lives in sql/21 (curated.reconciled_inventory); sql/20 is its reference matview (producing_reference, the ±150 ft corridor). Both were read.

Verdict

Not a defect in the sql/21 overlap rule. No change to the matching logic is warranted. Three findings, in order of size:
  1. Most of the 275 neighbours are not producers. 172 of 275 (63 %) have no first production: 73 cancelled permits, 56 approved permits not yet drilled, 41 spud or completed-not-yet-producing, 2 abandoned. Only 103 are producing wells, and only 11 of the 63 within 200 ft. A PUD 33 ft from a cancelled permit is remaining inventory, not a realized one.
  2. The one real problem is staleness, not logic. The stored reconciled_inventory was last built about 2026-09-19. producing_reference and formation_blueox_tvd refresh nightly; the reconciliation does not. Re-running the sql/21 body on live inputs changes 75 of 3,425 WCA PUDs, 42 of them to realized_phantom (31 of those are in the BOX 1,934 covered set). Eleven of the 275 are among them. A plain REFRESH fixes it; that is a data write on the shared DB, so it needs your go.
  3. The remaining 77 are the corridor working as designed. Fully co-extent producers sitting 157 to over 400 ft off the PUD stick, outside the ±150 ft corridor by construction. Whether ±150 ft is too tight for WCA is a calibration question; the evidence here does not settle it.

Evidence on whether ±150 ft is too tight:

  • For widening: the 93 stored realized WCA matches have offsets (to the actual polyline) of 0–50 ft: 10; 50–100: 27; 100–125: 15; 125–150: 16; > 150: 25 (curved sticks that pass on overlap). The density does not taper toward the cut, so a tail beyond 150 ft is plausible.
  • Against: Novi never puts two PUDs within 500 ft of each other (0 of 1,259), so a PUD at 150–300 ft from a producer is either Novi's misplaced twin of that well or very tight infill. Beyond 400 ft (35 of 77) it is a normal half-spacing slot. The producer-to-PUD gap distribution rises smoothly from 200 to 1,000 ft with no break to calibrate against.

The 7 pairs reaching 50 % at ±200 ft and 17 at ±250 ft are the only plausible misses; ±300 ft adds 27 and starts to capture real half-spacing infill. A corridor change needs a labelled sample of known-drilled slots versus Novi stick offset. This audit cannot justify one.

4. The stored reconciled_inventory is stale

pg_stat_user_tables: reconciled_inventory last autovacuum 2026-09-19 21:37 UTC (about its build); producing_reference refreshed nightly (latest 2026-10-14), formation_blueox_tvd likewise. The sql/21 header says "refresh as new wells come online", but it is quarterly-owned and not in etl/db.py:_CURATED_MATVIEWS, so nothing does.

The sql/21 body was re-run read-only (SELECT, identical predicate and intel_vintage_date()) for all 3,425 Delaware WCA_1/WCA_2 PUDs the BOX universe starts from. 3,350 of 3,425 (97.8 %) reproduce the stored status exactly, so the logic is the same; the 75 differences are changed inputs:

The cause is not new first production. The 42 newly realized match producers whose first production runs 2015-03 to 2026-08 (25 before 2025), and 41 of the 42 pass through the ≤ 150 ft depth clause. For 24 of them the stored row had no candidate above 5 % overlap at all, so the producer's stick or TVD was not usable at build time. That is consistent with wells geometry and TVD back-fills landing in the nightlies after 09-19; the specific upstream change cannot be isolated without build history.

Consequence for BOX: 31 of the 1,934 covered PUDs are forecast although a live re-run of sql/21 calls them already drilled (realized_phantom: a producer already occupies the slot).

5. Recommendation

  1. Do not change sql/21's matching rule. No logic defect found; the ±150 ft corridor and TVD guard reproduce and behave as documented. Rule 9 stands.
  2. Refresh curated.reconciled_inventory (data, not DDL): needs your explicit go-apply. REFRESH MATERIALIZED VIEW CONCURRENTLY (UNIQUE index present). Quarterly-owned, so follow with net_new_pdp and erebor_locations (it carries the status) in chain order, then re-annotate BOX. Separately decide whether reconciliation should be refreshed on a cadence: the header intends it, nothing implements it. That would be a _CURATED_MATVIEWS change under the warehouse-change skill.
  3. Leave the 77 P3 and 11 P2 flagged (keep forecast, flag). They are not realized under the rule of record. Optional separate piece: calibrate the corridor.
  4. BOX side, not warehouse: restrict the "possible realized PUD (reconciliation)" flag to neighbours with a first production date. As built it fires 172 times on permits and spuds. Give the 41 in-flight wells their own text ("well spud/completed in this slot"); 12 of them cover ≥ 50 %.
  5. Optional, later: realized_inflight in sql/21 for spud/completed wells without production. Small count, a scope decision, warehouse-change skill, explicit go.

6. Outside the question

BOX D64 spacing includes non-producers. Of the 623 covered locations whose nearest neighbour is a pdp: stick, 295 (47 %) resolve to a permit, spud or cancelled well. Those gaps feed spacing_ft / planned_spacing_ft, an input to the forecast and not only to the flag. A cancelled permit at 33 ft is not a neighbour. I did not touch it; raise it in the BOX session. (Nearest-neighbour types over the 1,882 covered locations that have one: 1,259 PUD, 328 producer, 295 non-producer.)

Method

  • Pairs: 275 from run.pkl (near_pdp_ft < 500; 63 under 200 ft); neighbour from nearest = pdp:<api10>. The annotated list regenerates with python -m scripts.box_pud_run annotate.
  • Per pair (read-only SELECTs through the ETL connection, SET TRANSACTION READ ONLY, rolled back): PUD stick, TVD, code (intel_locations, intel_formation_blueox); stored reconciled_inventory row; neighbour's wells row and producing_reference membership; formation_blueox_tvd corrected code; the ±46 m overlap exactly as sql/21 computes it. Overlaps at 100–400 ft, axial span and azimuth on projected sticks (box.production.project_ft).
  • Live re-run: the sql/21 lateral subquery restricted to the listed sticks (275-row and 3,425-row passes).
  • Caveats: BOX gap is a straight-stick approximation; "within 500 ft" is BOX's cutoff, not a realization test. P3 sensitivity counts pairs, one per PUD. Stale-cause attribution is inferential (no build history).
